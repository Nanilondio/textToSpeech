Making a Complaint

An intermediate phone-conversation focused on polite-but-firm complaints and problem resolution. Vocabulary includes 'apologise', 'refund', 'compensation', and 'manager'.

Level B1 Complaints & Customer Service ⏱ 3 minutes

Vocabulary

complaint a statement that you are unhappy about something
apologise to say you are sorry for something you have done wrong
refund money that is returned to you because you are not satisfied
compensation something, usually money, given to make up for a problem
resolve to find a solution to a problem

Dialogue

Speaker 1 Good evening. This is the Lake View Hotel. How can I help you?
Speaker 2 Good evening. I am calling to make a complaint about room 312.
Speaker 1 I am very sorry to hear that. Can you tell me what happened?
Speaker 2 The room was extremely noisy because of construction work next door, even at midnight.
Speaker 1 I sincerely apologise for that. That is not the experience we want for our guests.
Speaker 2 I appreciate that. What kind of compensation can you offer?
Speaker 1 We can offer you a full refund for last night and a free dinner tonight.
Speaker 2 That is fair. Can I also move to a quieter room for the rest of my stay?
Speaker 1 Of course. I will move you to a room on the top floor, away from the street.
Speaker 2 Thank you. I am happy with this solution.

Comprehension Questions

1. Why is the customer complaining?
  1. The room was dirty
  2. The room was noisy
  3. The food was cold
The room was noisy
2. What compensation does the hotel offer?
  1. A free breakfast
  2. A refund and a free dinner
  3. A discount on the next visit
A refund and a free dinner
3. Where will the customer be moved?
  1. To a room on the ground floor
  2. To a room on the top floor
  3. To another hotel
To a room on the top floor

True / False

The construction work happened only during the day.
False — The customer says the noise was 'even at midnight,' so it was happening at night too.
The hotel refuses to apologise.
False — The receptionist says 'I sincerely apologise for that.'

Teacher Tips

  • Pre-teach the contrast between polite and rude complaints. Use the dialogue as the polite model.
  • Drill formulaic phrases: 'I am calling to make a complaint…', 'I appreciate that…', 'I am happy with…'
  • After listening, students write a short formal complaint email about a real situation (school, café, gym).

Presentar una queja

Una conversación telefónica intermedia centrada en quejas educadas pero firmes y en la resolución de problemas. El vocabulario incluye 'apologise', 'refund', 'compensation' y 'manager'.

Nivel B1 Quejas y atención al cliente ⏱ 3 minutes

Vocabulario

complaint una declaración de que no estás contento con algo
apologise decir que sientes haber hecho algo mal
refund dinero que se te devuelve porque no estás satisfecho
compensation algo, normalmente dinero, que se da para compensar un problema
resolve encontrar una solución a un problema

Diálogo

Speaker 1 Good evening. This is the Lake View Hotel. How can I help you?
Speaker 2 Good evening. I am calling to make a complaint about room 312.
Speaker 1 I am very sorry to hear that. Can you tell me what happened?
Speaker 2 The room was extremely noisy because of construction work next door, even at midnight.
Speaker 1 I sincerely apologise for that. That is not the experience we want for our guests.
Speaker 2 I appreciate that. What kind of compensation can you offer?
Speaker 1 We can offer you a full refund for last night and a free dinner tonight.
Speaker 2 That is fair. Can I also move to a quieter room for the rest of my stay?
Speaker 1 Of course. I will move you to a room on the top floor, away from the street.
Speaker 2 Thank you. I am happy with this solution.

Preguntas de comprensión

1. ¿Por qué se queja el cliente?
  1. La habitación estaba sucia
  2. La habitación era ruidosa
  3. La comida estaba fría
La habitación era ruidosa
2. ¿Qué compensación ofrece el hotel?
  1. Un desayuno gratis
  2. Un reembolso y una cena gratis
  3. Un descuento en la próxima visita
Un reembolso y una cena gratis
3. ¿Adónde trasladarán al cliente?
  1. A una habitación en la planta baja
  2. A una habitación en la última planta
  3. A otro hotel
A una habitación en la última planta

Verdadero / Falso

Las obras solo ocurrían durante el día.
Falso — El cliente dice que el ruido era 'even at midnight', así que también ocurría por la noche.
El hotel se niega a disculparse.
Falso — El recepcionista dice 'I sincerely apologise for that'.

Consejos para el docente

  • Enseña el contraste entre quejas educadas y groseras. Usa el diálogo como modelo educado.
  • Practica fórmulas fijas: 'I am calling to make a complaint…', 'I appreciate that…', 'I am happy with…'.
  • Después de escuchar, los estudiantes escriben un correo formal corto de queja sobre una situación real (escuela, cafetería, gimnasio).

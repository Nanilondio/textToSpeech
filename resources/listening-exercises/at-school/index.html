At School

A short classroom-themed dialogue that recycles school vocabulary: subjects, teachers, classrooms, break, lunch. Useful as the first lesson of a new school year.

Level A1 School & Classes ⏱ 1–2 minutes

Vocabulary

subject a topic you study at school, like math or history
favourite the one you like most
teacher the adult who teaches you at school
break a short time between lessons when you can relax
classroom the room where you have lessons

Dialogue

Speaker 1 What is your favourite subject, Lily?
Speaker 2 I like art. My art teacher is very nice. What about you?
Speaker 1 I really like science. The teacher is funny.
Speaker 2 Where is your classroom?
Speaker 1 It is on the second floor. And yours?
Speaker 2 Mine is on the first floor, next to the library.
Speaker 1 Do you have a long break?
Speaker 2 Yes, we have a fifteen-minute break at ten o'clock.

  1. Science
  2. Art
  3. Math
Art
2. Where is the speaker's classroom?
  1. On the first floor
  2. On the second floor
  3. Next to the library
On the second floor
3. How long is the break?
  1. Ten minutes
  2. Fifteen minutes
  3. Twenty minutes
Fifteen minutes

True / False

Lily likes science.
False — Lily says she likes art. The speaker likes science.
The break is at ten o'clock.
True — Lily says 'we have a fifteen-minute break at ten o'clock.'

Teacher Tips

  • Pre-teach the names of school subjects with flashcards before listening.
  • After listening, students rank the subjects mentioned from 'most useful' to 'most fun' in pairs.
  • Extension: learners ask and answer 'What is your favourite subject?' around the class.

En la escuela

Un diálogo corto sobre el colegio que recicla vocabulario escolar: asignaturas, profesores, aulas, recreo, almuerzo. Útil como primera clase de un nuevo curso.

Nivel A1 Escuela y clases ⏱ 1–2 minutes

Vocabulario

subject un tema que estudias en la escuela, como matemáticas o historia
favourite el que más te gusta
teacher el adulto que te enseña en la escuela
break un tiempo corto entre clases para descansar
classroom la sala donde tienes las clases

Preguntas de comprensión

1. ¿Cuál es la asignatura favorita de Lily?
  1. Ciencias
  2. Arte
  3. Matemáticas
Arte
2. ¿Dónde está el aula del interlocutor?
  1. En la primera planta
  2. En la segunda planta
  3. Al lado de la biblioteca
En la segunda planta
3. ¿Cuánto dura el recreo?
  1. Diez minutos
  2. Quince minutos
  3. Veinte minutos
Quince minutos

Verdadero / Falso

A Lily le gustan las ciencias.
Falso — Lily dice que le gusta el arte. Al interlocutor le gustan las ciencias.
El recreo es a las diez.
Verdadero — Lily dice 'we have a fifteen-minute break at ten o'clock'.

Consejos para el docente

  • Enseña antes los nombres de las asignaturas con tarjetas.
  • Después de escuchar, los estudiantes ordenan las asignaturas mencionadas de 'más útil' a 'más divertida' por parejas.
  • Ampliación: los estudiantes preguntan y responden 'What is your favourite subject?' por la clase.

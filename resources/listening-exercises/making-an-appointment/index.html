Making an Appointment

An elementary phone-conversation dialogue. Learners hear days of the week, times of day, and useful phrases for confirming, refusing, and rescheduling appointments.

Level A2 Appointments & Services ⏱ 2 minutes

Vocabulary

appointment an arranged time to meet someone, usually for a service
available free to do something at a particular time
schedule a plan of times when things will happen
reschedule to arrange a new time for something
confirm to say that something is definitely true or arranged

Dialogue

Speaker 1 Good morning, City Dental Clinic. How can I help you?
Speaker 2 Hello, I would like to make an appointment with Doctor Lee.
Speaker 1 Sure. Are you available this Thursday afternoon?
Speaker 2 I am afraid not. I work on Thursday afternoons.
Speaker 1 No problem. What about Friday morning at ten?
Speaker 2 Friday at ten is perfect. Is the clinic easy to find?
Speaker 1 Yes, we are on Park Avenue, next to the post office.
Speaker 2 Great. Can I confirm the appointment for Friday at ten?
Speaker 1 Yes, confirmed. We will see you on Friday.

  2. Doctor Park
  3. The receptionist

2. Why can't the caller come on Thursday?
  1. They are travelling
  2. They work on Thursdays
  3. They are sick
They work on Thursdays
3. Where is the clinic?
  1. On King Street
  2. On Park Avenue
  3. On Main Road
On Park Avenue

True / False

The appointment is for Friday morning.
True — The caller and the receptionist agree on Friday at ten.
The clinic is next to the bank.
False — The receptionist says the clinic is 'next to the post office.'

Teacher Tips

  • Drill 'I am afraid not' and 'That works for me' as fixed phrases for politely accepting or refusing offers.
  • Practise the phone opening: 'Good morning, [company]. How can I help you?'
  • Follow-up writing activity: students write a short email confirming an appointment.

Pedir una cita

Un diálogo elemental por teléfono. Los estudiantes oyen días de la semana, partes del día y frases útiles para confirmar, rechazar y reprogramar citas.

Nivel A2 Citas y servicios ⏱ 2 minutes

Vocabulario

appointment un horario acordado para encontrarse con alguien, normalmente para un servicio
available libre para hacer algo en un momento concreto
schedule un plan de horarios en los que sucederán cosas
reschedule establecer un nuevo horario para algo
confirm decir que algo es definitivamente cierto o está acordado

Diálogo

Speaker 1 Good morning, City Dental Clinic. How can I help you?
Speaker 2 Hello, I would like to make an appointment with Doctor Lee.
Speaker 1 Sure. Are you available this Thursday afternoon?
Speaker 2 I am afraid not. I work on Thursday afternoons.
Speaker 1 No problem. What about Friday morning at ten?
Speaker 2 Friday at ten is perfect. Is the clinic easy to find?
Speaker 1 Yes, we are on Park Avenue, next to the post office.
Speaker 2 Great. Can I confirm the appointment for Friday at ten?
Speaker 1 Yes, confirmed. We will see you on Friday.

Preguntas de comprensión

1. ¿A quién quiere ver el llamante?
  1. Doctor Lee
  2. Doctor Park
  3. Al recepcionista
Doctor Lee
2. ¿Por qué no puede venir el jueves?
  1. Está de viaje
  2. Trabaja los jueves
  3. Está enfermo
Trabaja los jueves
3. ¿Dónde está la clínica?
  1. En King Street
  2. En Park Avenue
  3. En Main Road
En Park Avenue

Verdadero / Falso

La cita es el viernes por la mañana.
Verdadero — El llamante y la recepcionista acuerdan el viernes a las diez.
La clínica está al lado del banco.
Falso — La recepcionista dice que la clínica está 'next to the post office'.

Consejos para el docente

  • Practica 'I am afraid not' y 'That works for me' como frases fijas para aceptar o rechazar ofertas con educación.
  • Practica la apertura por teléfono: 'Good morning, [company]. How can I help you?'.
  • Actividad de escritura de seguimiento: los estudiantes escriben un correo corto confirmando una cita.

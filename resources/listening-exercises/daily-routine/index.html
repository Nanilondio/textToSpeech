Daily Routine

A beginner-friendly monologue with short conversational interjections. Learners hear sequencing words (then, after that, in the evening) and time expressions (at seven o'clock, at half past eight).

Level A1 Daily Routine ⏱ 1–2 minutes

Vocabulary

wake up to stop sleeping and open your eyes
have breakfast to eat the first meal of the day
usually something you do most days
after later than something; following it in time
go to bed to lie down to sleep at night

Dialogue

Speaker 1 So, Tom, what time do you usually wake up?
Speaker 2 I wake up at seven o'clock on weekdays.
Speaker 1 And what do you do first?
Speaker 2 I have breakfast with my family. Then I brush my teeth.
Speaker 1 When do you go to school?
Speaker 2 I go to school at half past eight. I take the bus.
Speaker 1 And after school?
Speaker 2 After school, I do my homework and then I have dinner. I go to bed at ten.

Comprehension Questions

1. When does Tom wake up on weekdays?
  1. At six o'clock
  2. At seven o'clock
  3. At eight o'clock
At seven o'clock
2. How does Tom go to school?
  1. On foot
  2. By bus
  3. By car
By bus
3. What does Tom do first in the morning?
  1. Brushes his teeth
  2. Has breakfast
  3. Takes the bus
Has breakfast

True / False

Tom wakes up at seven on weekends too.
False — Tom says he wakes up at seven 'on weekdays'.
Tom does his homework before dinner.
True — He says 'After school, I do my homework and then I have dinner.'

Teacher Tips

  • Drill the time expressions (at seven o'clock, at half past eight) before listening.
  • Have students fill in a daily-routine chart while listening — first column for Tom, second column for themselves.
  • Use this as a follow-up writing prompt: 'Write five sentences about your daily routine.'

Rutina diaria

Un monólogo apto para principiantes con breves intervenciones conversacionales. Los estudiantes oyen palabras de secuenciación (then, after that, in the evening) y expresiones de tiempo (at seven o'clock, at half past eight).

Nivel A1 Rutina diaria ⏱ 1–2 minutes

Vocabulario

wake up dejar de dormir y abrir los ojos
have breakfast comer la primera comida del día
usually algo que haces casi todos los días
after más tarde que algo; siguiéndolo en el tiempo
go to bed tumbarse para dormir por la noche

Diálogo

Speaker 1 So, Tom, what time do you usually wake up?
Speaker 2 I wake up at seven o'clock on weekdays.
Speaker 1 And what do you do first?
Speaker 2 I have breakfast with my family. Then I brush my teeth.
Speaker 1 When do you go to school?
Speaker 2 I go to school at half past eight. I take the bus.
Speaker 1 And after school?
Speaker 2 After school, I do my homework and then I have dinner. I go to bed at ten.

Preguntas de comprensión

1. ¿A qué hora se despierta Tom los días laborables?
  1. A las seis
  2. A las siete
  3. A las ocho
A las siete
2. ¿Cómo va Tom al colegio?
  1. A pie
  2. En autobús
  3. En coche
En autobús
3. ¿Qué hace Tom primero por la mañana?
  1. Se lava los dientes
  2. Desayuna
  3. Toma el autobús
Desayuna

Verdadero / Falso

Tom se despierta a las siete también los fines de semana.
Falso — Tom dice que se despierta a las siete 'on weekdays' (los días laborables).
Tom hace los deberes antes de cenar.
Verdadero — Dice 'After school, I do my homework and then I have dinner'.

Consejos para el docente

  • Practica antes las expresiones de tiempo (at seven o'clock, at half past eight).
  • Pide a los estudiantes que completen una tabla de rutina mientras escuchan — primera columna para Tom, segunda para ellos mismos.
  • Usa esto como ejercicio de escritura: 'Escribe cinco frases sobre tu rutina diaria'.
